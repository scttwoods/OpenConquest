How to Play

Take every city. Cities build units — tap one of yours to choose what. Tap a unit to select it (it stays highlighted); tap it again to deselect. With a unit selected, tap a destination — a neighboring tile moves/attacks at once, a far tile sets a path (drawn as a line) the unit follows over several turns. Your own units can stack together on any tile, so tapping a tile that already holds a friendly unit moves onto it. To select a unit on such a tile instead of moving onto it, shift-click or right-click it (or open the stack panel via its count badge). Press and hold anywhere to measure distance — handy for checking whether a Fighter can reach a target and still return. To change a plan, select the unit and tap a new destination, or tap Cancel Move to stop it. Units that have finished acting this turn are dimmed. Armies capture cities (about half of assaults succeed), and capturing one prompts you to choose what it builds. Fighters fly anywhere but must refuel at cities or Carriers. To ship Armies across the sea, put them in a coastal city with a Transport, select the Transport and tap Load (or use Board in the stack panel), then sail. When several units share a tile it shows a count badge — tap it to open a panel listing each unit's state and pick one. A little roof marker in a tile's corner means a city sits underneath.

n next unit · space skip · s sleep · p patrol · Enter end turn · Esc deselect · shift/right-click select · hold measure · drag/arrows pan · wheel or pinch zoom · tap minimap to jump

Patrol: select a unit, press p, click waypoints along the route, then Enter. It loops the route until it spots an enemy or you give a new order.

Auto: On skips turns where nothing needs orders — the game fast-forwards to your next available action (like waiting for your first Army to finish).
