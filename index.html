How to Play

Take every city. Cities build units — tap one of yours to choose what. Tap a unit to select it (it stays highlighted); tap it again to deselect. Tap another of your units to switch to it. With a unit selected, tap a destination — a neighboring tile moves/attacks at once, a far tile sets a path (drawn as a line) the unit follows over several turns. Units that have finished acting this turn are dimmed. Armies capture cities (about half of assaults succeed). Fighters fly anywhere but must refuel at cities or Carriers. Armies board an adjacent Transport to cross the sea. When several units share a tile it shows a count badge — tap it to open a panel listing each unit's state and pick one.

n next unit · space skip · s sentry · Enter end turn · Esc deselect · drag/arrows pan · wheel or pinch zoom · tap minimap to jump

Auto: On skips turns where nothing needs orders — the game fast-forwards to your next available action (like waiting for your first Army to finish).
